# Burke-Litwin Causal Model - questionnaire

To run a Burke-Litwin Causal Model diagnostic, use this step-by-step questionnaire to evaluate your organization across its 12 causal dimensions, scoring alignment and health from 1 (Strong Misalignment/Dysfunctional) to 5 (Strong Alignment/Optimal).

## Step 1: Input (External Driver)

1.  External Environment

- Question: How well does leadership monitor and respond to external shifts (market trends, client demands, technology, regulations, and competitors)?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

## Step 2: Transformational Factors (Deep Culture & Direction)

1.  Mission and Strategy

- Question: Is our core purpose and strategic direction clear, inspiring, and understood by everyone in the organization?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

2.  Leadership

- Question: Do executive leaders model the behaviors, values, and accountability they expect from the rest of the company?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

3.  Organizational Culture

- Question: Do our unwritten rules, daily norms, and shared values foster trust, collaboration, and high performance rather than politics or fear?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

## Step 3: Transactional Factors (Everyday Operations & Climate)

1.  Structure

- Question: Are our reporting lines and functional boundaries clear, allowing decisions to be made without excessive bureaucracy?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

2.  Management Practices

- Question: Do middle managers allocate resources, plan workloads, and coordinate projects effectively to support the strategy?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

3.  Systems (Policies & Procedures)

- Question: Do our standard operating procedures, HR systems, and IT tools facilitate smooth workflows rather than adding friction?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

4.  Work Unit Climate

- Question: Do individual teams experience psychological safety, cooperation, and positive working relationships day-to-day?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

5.  Task Requirements & Individual Skills

- Question: Do employees have the exact training, competencies, and tools required to perform their specific job duties successfully?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

6.  Individual Needs & Values

- Question: Do our reward, recognition, and workplace flexibility structures match what our employees personally value and need to feel fulfilled?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

7.  Motivation

- Question: Are employees genuinely energized, engaged, and willing to go above and beyond in their daily work?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

## Step 4: Output (Performance Result)

1.  Individual and Organizational Performance

- Question: Are we consistently hitting our key performance indicators (productivity, quality, retention, and profitability goals)?
  - Rating (1–5): [ ]

## Step 5: Causal Analysis

- Trace the Cascading Flow: Look upstream. If Performance (12) or Motivation (11) is low, trace backward to see if the breakdown originates in transactional elements (Climate/Systems) or transformational root causes (Culture/Leadership).
- Target the Right Level: Do not try to fix a low score in Culture (4) with a minor tweak to Structure (5). Transformational gaps require executive-level interventions.
